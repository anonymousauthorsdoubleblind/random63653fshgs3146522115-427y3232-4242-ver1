Review materials

Source code, results tables and interactive replays for a computational study of how to choose the points of a reaction energy surface. The study uses the machine-learned density functional Skala-1.1 on two hydrogen-shift reactions, vinyl alcohol → acetaldehyde and formamide → formimidic acid. These pages are for reviewers. They hold the records behind the tables, not the manuscript.
